Why use an agent instead of applying directly?

Applying for a Japanese student visa requires 20+ unique documents, almost all in Japanese. Certified agents don't charge you a fee — the schools pay them — so you get free immigration document checking, housing assistance, and community support with no markup. For a first-time applicant, that's months of headaches removed.

Common questions

Q. Do study-abroad agents charge students a fee?
A. No. Reputable Japanese language-school agents are paid by the partner schools, so document checking, school selection, and housing help are free to you, and the consultation does not commit you to anything.

Q. How many documents does a Japanese student visa need?
A. A first-time student-visa application needs 20+ unique documents, almost all in Japanese. A certified agent prepares the Certificate of Eligibility (COE) that actually secures the visa.

Q. How long is a typical language-school program?
A. The classic route to fluency is 6 months to 2 years at an accredited school on a student visa, with the agent matching you by city, budget, and goal such as a university pathway or work.

Q. What if I am not ready to move to Japan yet?
A. Keep momentum with online 1-on-1 lessons on italki or Preply. The biggest drop-off after self-study is never speaking out loud, and a 30-minute trial exposes the gap between recognizing and producing words.
