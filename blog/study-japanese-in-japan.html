The options, in detail

THE FULL MOVE

Long-term language school via an agent

The classic route to fluency: 6 months to 2 years at an accredited Japanese language school on a student visa. A good agent matches you to a school by city, budget, and goal (university pathway vs. work), then handles the Certificate of Eligibility — the document that actually gets you the visa.

Online lessons with a native tutor

Why use an agent instead of applying directly?

Applying for a Japanese student visa requires 20+ unique documents, almost all in Japanese. Certified agents don't charge you a fee — the schools pay them — so you get free immigration document checking, housing assistance, and community support with no markup. For a first-time applicant, that's months of headaches removed.
