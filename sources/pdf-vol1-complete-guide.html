Which Visa Do I Need? — Decision Tree

START HERE — FIND YOUR VISA CATEGORY
Are you married to a Japanese national or permanent resident?
→ YES: Spouse or Child of Japanese National (配偶者等) — you/your spouse file a Certificate of Eligibility (在留資格認定証明書) at Immigration in Japan; the embassy only issues the visa AFTER the COE is approved (if you're already in Japan on another status, apply for a 在留資格変更 at Immigration)
→ NO: Continue ↓
Do you have a job offer from a Japanese employer?
→ YES: Continue ↓
→ NO: Are you enrolling in a school or university?
→ YES: Student (留学) — school applies for your COE
→ NO: Check Working Holiday eligibility (usually ages 18–30, but some countries are 18–25 — check your nationality at mofa.go.jp)
What industry / role is your Japan job in?
→ Engineering, IT, Finance, Legal, Translation, Accounting, Education, Design: Engineer / Specialist in Humanities / International Services — most common work visa
→ Restaurant, Hotel, Agriculture, Caregiving, Construction (certified roles): Specified Skilled Worker Type 1 (特定技能1号) — employer must be registered
→ Management / Company Representative: Business Manager (経営・管理) — requires registered company address in Japan
→ Academic research / Professor: Professor (教授) or Researcher (研究) — university or research institution applies

The First Two Weeks — Unlock Sequence

Every bureaucratic step in Japan's first weeks depends on another step. Follow this sequence exactly to break the cycle efficiently.

1
Airport Immigration → Collect Residence Card (在留カード) Issued on arrival at major airports (Narita, Haneda, Kansai, Chubu, New Chitose, Hiroshima, Fukuoka). Address field is blank — this is normal. Without this card, nothing else is possible.
↓
2
City Hall (市区町村役場) → Register Address (転入届) Within 14 days of moving in. Bring: Residence Card + Passport + Lease agreement. Address gets stamped on Residence Card. Enroll in health insurance and pension at the same visit. Deadline: 14 days (legal requirement).
↓
3
SIM Card → Get a Japanese Phone Number Requires: Residence Card with address + payment method. IIJmio, Rakuten Mobile, or Mineo are most accessible for newcomers. Phone number needed for bank verification.
↓
4
Japan Post Bank (ゆうちょ銀行) → Open Bank Account Available at all post offices. Most foreigner-accessible option. Requires: Residence Card + Passport. Avoid major city banks until after 3–6 months of residency.
↓
5
Utilities → Gas, Water, Electricity Contact each utility company by phone or online. Scripts below. Gas requires an in-person technician visit — schedule within your first week as cooking depends on it.

City Hall Scripts — Word for Word

These scripts are designed for a single visit to complete all registration tasks. Show the Japanese text directly to the counter staff if needed.

STEP 1 — ENTERING THE BUILDING
外国人登録の窓口はどこですか？ Gaikokujin tōroku no madoguchi wa doko desu ka? Where is the counter for foreign resident registration?
番号札はどこで取ればいいですか？ Bangō-fuda wa doko de toreba ii desu ka? Where do I take a number ticket?
STEP 2 — ADDRESS REGISTRATION (転入届)
転入届を出したいのですが。引っ越してきたばかりです。 Tennyūtodoke o dashitai no desu ga. Hikkoshite kita bakari desu. I'd like to file my move-in registration. I just moved here.
日本語があまりわかりません。英語の書類はありますか？ Nihongo ga amari wakarimasen. Eigo no shorui wa arimasu ka? I don't understand much Japanese. Are there English documents?
これが賃貸契約書です。 Kore ga chintai keiyaku-sho desu. This is my rental agreement.
STEP 3 — HEALTH INSURANCE (国民健康保険)
国民健康保険の加入手続きもお願いしたいのですが。 Kokumin kenkō hoken no kanyū tetsuzuki mo onegai shitai no desu ga. I'd also like to enroll in National Health Insurance.
勤め先の健康保険には加入していません。 Tsutomesaki no kenkō hoken ni wa kanyū shite imasen. I am not enrolled in employer health insurance.
STEP 4 — NATIONAL PENSION (国民年金)
国民年金の加入手続きもお願いします。 Kokumin nenkin no kanyū tetsuzuki mo onegai shimasu. Please also help me enroll in National Pension.
支払いが難しい場合、免除制度はありますか？ Shiharai ga muzukashii baai, menjo seido wa arimasu ka? If payment is difficult, is there a waiver/exemption system?
UTILITIES — CALLING THE GAS COMPANY
ガスの開栓をお願いしたいのですが。新しく引っ越してきました。 Gasu no kaisen o onegai shitai no desu ga. Atarashiku hikkoshite kimashita. I'd like to have the gas turned on. I just moved in.
住所は___です。 Jūsho wa ___ desu. My address is ___. (Read from your lease)
立ち会いはいつ可能ですか？ Tachiai wa itsu kanō desu ka? When is an in-person inspection possible?

Key questions to ask the agent (仲介業者)

AT THE REAL ESTATE AGENT
外国人でも入居できますか？ Gaikokujin demo nyūkyo dekimasu ka? Can foreigners rent here?
礼金なしの物件はありますか？ Reikin nashi no bukken wa arimasu ka? Do you have any apartments with no key money?
家賃を下げることはできますか？ Yachin o sageru koto wa dekimasu ka? Is there any room to negotiate the rent?
保証人なしで入居できる保証会社はありますか？ Hoshōnin nashi de nyūkyo dekiru hoshō-gaisha wa arimasu ka? Is there a guarantee company available so I don't need a personal guarantor?
過去に何か問題はありましたか？（孤独死・事故物件など） Kako ni nanika mondai wa arimashita ka? (Kodokushi / jiko bukken nado) Were there any incidents in the past? (Lonely death, accident, etc.) — Agent is legally required to disclose known events.

Garbage collection — quick reference

CATEGORYJAPANESEWHAT GOES INNOTES
Combustible燃えるごみFood waste, paper, fabric, leather, rubberMost common — usually 2×/week
Non-combustible燃えないごみGlass, ceramics, small metal items, small appliancesUsually 1–2×/month
Plastic containersプラスチック / 容器包装Plastic packaging marked with the recycling symbol — rinse firstWeekly in most cities
Cans & Bottles缶・びんAluminum cans, steel cans, glass bottles — rinseUsually 1×/week
Paper / Cardboard古紙・段ボールNewspapers, flyers, cardboard — bundle and tieMonthly or by collection point
Large items粗大ごみFurniture, appliances, bicyclesBuy disposal ticket first (¥200–2,000), schedule pickup via city website
GET YOUR CITY'S APP Most Japanese cities have a garbage sorting app (e.g., "さんあ~る" from the Ministry of Environment) that tells you exactly which day each category goes out in your specific address area. Download it at your city hall or search your city's name + "ゴミアプリ".

IC card setup — transit and payment

  • Suica (East Japan / Tokyo area) or Pasmo: get at any JR East or Tokyo Metro ticket machine
  • ICOCA (Osaka / Kansai area): at JR West machines
  • Top up (チャージ) at any IC machine, convenience store, or linked app
  • Works on: trains, subways, buses, taxis, convenience stores, vending machines, some restaurants
  • Smartphone-based IC cards (Suica on Apple/Google Pay) available — no physical card needed
