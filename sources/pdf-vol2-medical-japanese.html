How to Use This Guide

This guide is organized as a journey through the Japanese healthcare system: starting with booking by phone, through the clinic visit, to prescription pickup. Each section has complete scripts you can read aloud or show to a Japanese speaker on your phone screen.

  • Show the Japanese text directly to clinic staff if you're unsure of pronunciation
  • The emergency card (page 3) is designed to be shown or handed to emergency staff — print it separately or screenshot it
  • Romaji pronunciations follow Hepburn romanization; vowels are pronounced as in Italian (a=ah, i=ee, u=oo, e=eh, o=oh)

Understanding Your Insurance Card (保険証)

保険証 — National Health Insurance Card
氏名 (Name)Your name in Japanese
生年月日 (DOB)Year / Month / Day (Japanese era or Western)
記号・番号 (ID)Your insurance registration number
保険者名 (Insurer)Your city/ward office name
Present this card at every medical facility. Without it, you pay 100% upfront (claimable later). With it: 30% co-pay.
SITUATIONJAPANESE + ROMAJIENGLISH
Presenting card保険証をお持ちです。Hokenshō o o-mochi desu.I have my insurance card.
Forgot card今日は保険証を持ってきていません。Kyō wa hokenshō o motte kite imasen.I didn't bring my insurance card today.
New insurance先月、保険証が変わりました。Sengetsu, hokenshō ga kawarimashita.My insurance card changed last month.
Employer insurance社会保険に入っています。Shakai hoken ni haitte imasu.I have employer health insurance.

Booking a Clinic Appointment by Phone

Calling a Japanese clinic for the first time is one of the most stressful tasks for new residents. The script below covers the entire call. Read it aloud or show it to someone who can read Japanese and make the call for you.

PHONE BOOKING SCRIPT — READ ALOUD OR SHOW TO STAFF
YOU →
すみません、予約をしたいのですが。
Sumimasen, yoyaku o shitai no desu ga.
Excuse me, I'd like to make an appointment.
CLINIC →
どのような症状でしょうか？
Dono yōna shōjō deshō ka?
What are your symptoms?
YOU →
__________があります。（下の症状リストから選んでください）
__________ ga arimasu. (Choose from the symptom list below)
I have __________ . (fill in your symptom)
CLINIC →
初めてですか？
Hajimete desu ka?
Is this your first visit?
YOU →
はい、初めてです。 / いいえ、以前に来たことがあります。
Hai, hajimete desu. / Iie, izen ni kita koto ga arimasu.
Yes, first time. / No, I've been here before.
YOU →
外国人なのですが、英語を話せる先生はいますか？
Gaikokujin na no desu ga, eigo o hanaseru sensei wa imasu ka?
I'm a foreigner — do you have a doctor who speaks English?
YOU →
___曜日の___時は空いていますか？
___ yōbi no ___ ji wa aite imasu ka?
Is ___day at ___:00 available?
TIP Many clinics now offer online booking (ネット予約) via their websites or via apps like LINE. Check the clinic's website for an online reservation button before calling — it's much easier.

Pharmacy (薬局) — Prescription Pickup

PRESCRIPTION PICKUP SCRIPT
YOU →
処方箋を持ってきました。
Shohōsen o motte kimashita.
I've brought my prescription.
PHARMACIST →
保険証はお持ちですか？
Hokenshō wa o-mochi desu ka?
Do you have your insurance card?
YOU →
はい、どうぞ。
Hai, dōzo.
Yes, here you go.
SITUATIONJAPANESE + ROMAJIENGLISH
How to take it?この薬の飲み方を教えてください。Kono kusuri no nomikata o oshiete kudasai.Please explain how to take this medicine.
Side effects?副作用はありますか？Fukusayō wa arimasu ka?Are there any side effects?
With food?食前ですか、食後ですか？Shokuzen desu ka, shokugo desu ka?Before meals or after meals?
Can I drink alcohol?この薬を飲んでいる間、お酒は飲めますか？Kono kusuri o nonde iru aida, o-sake wa nomemasu ka?Can I drink alcohol while taking this?
English label?英語の説明書はありますか？Eigo no setsumeisho wa arimasu ka?Is there an English instruction leaflet?
OTC painkiller市販の鎮痛剤を買いたいのですが。Shihan no chintsu-zai o kaitai no desu ga.I'd like to buy an over-the-counter painkiller.
OTC cold medicine風邪薬をください。Kazegusuri o kudasai.I'd like cold medicine, please.

Mental Health and Psychological Support

Seeking mental health support is increasingly normalized in Japan. Clinics for 心療内科 (psychosomatic medicine) or 精神科 (psychiatry) are available in most cities.

SITUATIONJAPANESE + ROMAJIENGLISH
Anxiety / stress不安感やストレスが続いています。Fuankan ya sutoresu ga tsuzuite imasu.I've been experiencing anxiety and stress.
Feeling depressed気分が沈んでいます。Kibun ga shizunde imasu.I've been feeling low/depressed.
Can't sleepストレスで眠れません。Sutoresu de nemuremasen.Stress is affecting my sleep.
Want counselingカウンセリングを受けたいのですが。Kaunseringu o uketai no desu ga.I'd like to try counseling.
English counselor?英語が話せるカウンセラーはいますか？Eigo ga hanaseru kaunserā wa imasu ka?Is there an English-speaking counselor?
