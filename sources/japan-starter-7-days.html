Your 7-Day Japan Starter
One small step a day: learn your first Japanese and line up the move or the job. Every day points to a free NihongoHub resource — work through it at your own pace.

New to Japan or Japanese? Start here. Do one day at a time. Each day teaches one useful phrase and gives you one concrete thing to do — all free.

DAY1

Find your level

Take the 5-minute JLPT quiz so you know exactly where you stand — N5 to N1. You can't plan a route without a starting point.

はじめまして。Hajimemashite — Nice to meet you. Take the free quiz →
DAY2

Five survival phrases

Learn the handful of phrases that get you through a shop, a station, and a restaurant. Tap any spot in a guide to hear it spoken.

すみません。Sumimasen — Excuse me / Sorry. See the survival phrases →
DAY3

Pick your place

Where would you live, work, or travel? Explore all 47 prefectures — food, culture, cost of living, and the local Japanese you'll actually use there.

ここはどこですか？Koko wa doko desu ka? — Where is this? Explore the map →
DAY4

If you're moving

The first two weeks decide everything: residence card → city hall → bank, in the right order. The free survival kit walks you through it.

お願いします。Onegai shimasu — Please / I'd appreciate it. Open the survival kit →
DAY5

If you're going to work

The Specified Skilled Worker (Tokutei Ginou) route needs no degree — just a skills test and JFT-Basic / JLPT N4. Compare prefectures by their official minimum wage before you choose.

働きたいです。Hatarakitai desu — I want to work. Minimum wage by prefecture →
DAY6

Start speaking

The biggest drop-off after self-study is never speaking out loud. Book a cheap trial lesson with a native tutor, or shadow real videos, and feel the gap close.

もう一度お願いします。Mō ichido onegai shimasu — One more time, please. Ways to start speaking →
DAY7

Make it stick

Turn it into a habit: a quick daily quiz, level up your character across the 47-prefecture adventure, and keep a weekly note in your inbox.

がんばります。Ganbarimasu — I'll do my best. Start the 47-prefecture adventure →
