The 5-Step Unlock Sequence

Every step depends on the previous one. Follow this order to avoid wasting days.

1
Airport → Collect Residence Card (在留カード) Issued at immigration at major airports. Address field is blank — that's normal. This is your master key. Without this: nothing else works.
↓
2
City Hall → Register Address + Health Insurance + Pension Do all three in one visit. Deadline: 14 days from move-in date. Bring Residence Card + Passport + Lease. After this: your card has your address stamped on it.
↓
3
Get a SIM Card (Japanese phone number) IIJmio / Rakuten Mobile / Mineo. Needs: Residence Card with address. Phone number unlocks bank verification.
↓
4
Japan Post Bank → Open Account (ゆうちょ銀行) Available at every post office. Most foreigner-accessible. Needs: Residence Card + Passport.
↓
5
Utilities → Gas, Water, Electricity Gas requires in-person technician visit — call within day 1 or 2. Water and electricity can be set up online.
COMPLETE FIRST TWO WEEKS CHECKLIST
  • Collect Residence Card at airport immigration Major airports only: Narita, Haneda, Kansai, Chubu, New Chitose, Hiroshima, Fukuoka
  • Go to city hall within 14 days — register address (転入届)
  • At city hall: enroll in National Health Insurance (国民健康保険)
  • At city hall: enroll in National Pension (国民年金)
  • At city hall: pick up garbage sorting calendar (ゴミ収集カレンダー)
  • Get SIM card with Japanese phone number
  • Open Japan Post Bank account (ゆうちょ銀行) at a post office
  • Call gas company to schedule in-person activation
  • Set up water and electricity (usually online or by phone)
  • Register bicycle with police if purchased (防犯登録, ~¥600)
  • Sign up for city emergency / disaster alerts
  • Locate nearest evacuation point on city map

5 Phrases That Unlock City Hall

1 — FINDING THE RIGHT WINDOW 外国人登録の窓口はどこですか？ Gaikokujin tōroku no madoguchi wa doko desu ka? Where is the counter for foreign resident registration?
2 — ADDRESS REGISTRATION 転入届を出したいのですが。 Tenyūtodoke o dashitai no desu ga. I'd like to file my move-in registration.
3 — HEALTH INSURANCE 国民健康保険に加入したいのですが。 Kokumin kenkō hoken ni kanyū shitai no desu ga. I'd like to enroll in National Health Insurance.
4 — PENSION 国民年金の手続きもお願いします。 Kokumin nenkin no tetsuzuki mo onegai shimasu. Please also help me with National Pension enrollment.
5 — WHEN LOST / CONFUSED 日本語がわかりません。英語の案内はありますか？ Nihongo ga wakarimasen. Eigo no annai wa arimasu ka? I don't understand Japanese. Is there English guidance available?
