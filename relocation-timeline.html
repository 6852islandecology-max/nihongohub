    🏦 After you arrive: the bank-account barrier

    Major banks (MUFG / SMBC / Mizuho) commonly require 6+ months of residence before opening an account; Japan Post Bank (Yucho) is a common exception for newer arrivals. Expect hanko (seal), proof of address, and My Number requirements. (secondary sources; varies by branch)

    Many newcomers bridge the gap (deposits, proof of funds, receiving income) with a multi-currency account — sourced cost facts in track-f.json and the transfer-cost estimator.
