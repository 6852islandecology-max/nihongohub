Affiliate disclosure & partnering.

We keep this simple and honest: here's how affiliate links work on NihongoHub, and how to partner with us.

Affiliate disclosure

Some links on NihongoHub — mostly hotel and activity booking partners on our travel and prefecture pages — are affiliate links. If you book or buy through them, we may earn a small commission, at no extra cost to you.

  • We only point to things we'd genuinely use or recommend ourselves.
  • Affiliate links are marked (e.g. a "PR" tag) where they appear.
  • A commission never changes what we recommend or how we rank it.

This keeps the core learning content free while staying transparent about how the site is funded.

Partner with us
