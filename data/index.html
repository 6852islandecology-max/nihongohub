NihongoHub Open Data

Machine-readable, source-attributed datasets for people learning Japanese or traveling to / living in Japan. Every fact carries the public source it came from. Numeric claims carry a confidence level, and anything we could not verify is labeled as such rather than asserted.

Free to cite and reuse. When you cite, please attribute NihongoHub (nihongo-hub.com). Discovery card: /.well-known/mcp/server-card.json

Japan 47 Prefecture Dataset

Signature and lesser-known foods, cultural sights, sub-areas, and a 5-axis profile per prefecture. Nature score derived from GBIF biodiversity data.

/data/prefectures.json

International Money-Transfer Cost Facts

For remote workers moving income across borders. Anchored on World Bank Remittance Prices Worldwide channel averages; provider statements quoted from their own public pages. Try it live: Transfer Cost Estimator.

/data/track-f.json

Japan Rail Reachability Matrix

Approximate fastest rail times from Tokyo, Kyoto and Shin-Osaka to all 47 prefectures, for time-budget trip discovery ("I have 3 hours — where can I go?"). Deliberately rough (~±20%), with a JR Pass caveat. Try it live: Where Next?

/data/reachability.json

Moving to Japan — Neutral Procedural Facts

Certificate of Eligibility timelines, residence-card issuance, bank-account barriers. Each item names its source and confidence; no manufactured urgency. Try it live: Relocation Timeline Planner.
